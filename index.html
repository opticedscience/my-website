Welcome to my personal webpage!

Hello, I’m Wenbo. I am currently on assignment at Meta Reality Labs, where I contribute to scientific software and modeling efforts supporting complex imaging and measurement systems. Previously, I was a Research Engineer at Global Health Labs (GHL) in Bellevue, Washington, working on interdisciplinary instrumentation and sensor platforms for healthcare delivery in low- and middle-income countries. My work focused on the design and implementation of end-to-end measurement systems, combining scientific software development, data pipelines, optical modeling, and hardware–software integration. I have led development of Python-based automation frameworks, instrument control software, and analytical workflows for multi-sensor systems, translating physics-based models and signal-processing methods into robust, deployable tools used in real-world environments.
